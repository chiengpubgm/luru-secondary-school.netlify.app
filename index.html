About Luru Secondary School

A school built on discipline, curiosity and respect.

A message from the head teacher

Welcome to Luru. We prepare our students for exams and for life by teaching strong values, good study habits and care for others. We work closely with parents so every learner is supported at school and at home.

Our mission

To give every student a quality education that builds knowledge, character and confidence.

Our vision

Graduates who lead with integrity and serve their communities.

Our values

Integrity, hard work, teamwork and respect for others.

Admissions

Joining Luru takes four simple steps.

  1. Visit or call the school officeAsk for the admission form and a tour of the school.
  2. Submit the documentsPrevious school report, birth certificate and passport photos.
  3. Sit a short placement testThis helps us place your child in the right class.
  4. Pay fees and receive your Student IDPay online, then show your receipt at the office.
